Why this shape

  • Each goal is tied to a moment ("after lunch", "before breakfast"). Research on habit apps found this builds real habits better than plain reminders.
  • Habits take about 66 days to stick, and one missed day doesn't undo them. So the week view is forgiving: a rest day, no reset to zero.
  • Every goal points to Satvik: yoga → Videos, salad → Menu/Plans, habits → Divya's consultation.
  • The popup shows only on the first open of the day, never on top of another popup, and can be turned off in Settings. After closing it, goals stay one tap away in Profile.

What it needs

  • Ticks, streaks, tips, timings and reminders: no native change, so it can ship as an over-the-air update.
  • Tips and goal lists from the website admin, so the team can change them without an app update.
  • Automatic step counting would need a native module and a motion permission in the store build.

Guardrails

  • Wellness wording only: no medical claims, diagnoses or calorie targets (App Store 1.4.1, Play health policy).
  • Numbers follow public guidance: 30–45 min activity a day (ICMR-NIN 2024), water adjusted for climate.
  • Content reviewed by Divya before it goes live.
